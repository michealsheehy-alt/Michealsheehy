Building a Global KYC Operating Model

How common infrastructure can create consistency without erasing local regulatory context.

Micheal SheehyExecutive perspectives on payments, compliance and adaptive governance.← All insights

The challenge

Global businesses often accumulate separate customer workflows, local systems and manual exceptions. The result is difficult to govern, expensive to change and inconsistent for customers.

The strategic decision

Treat customer lifecycle management as shared infrastructure rather than a collection of country projects. Define common customer objects, evidence standards, risk concepts and governance before configuring local requirements.

The operating model

Central teams own architecture, policy and change governance. Regional experts interpret local requirements, validate outcomes and escalate ambiguity. Product, technology, operations and compliance share accountability for the end-to-end journey.

The lesson

Global consistency is not achieved by eliminating local judgment. It is achieved by making local judgment visible, governed and reusable across one platform.
