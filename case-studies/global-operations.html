Scaling Compliance Without Creating a Global Bottleneck

Designing a distributed organization that increases resilience while preserving one standard.

Micheal SheehyExecutive perspectives on payments, compliance and adaptive governance.← All insights

The challenge

Centralized teams can become slow, distant from customers and dependent on a small number of locations. Distributed teams can fragment standards and create duplicated leadership.

The strategic decision

Build global hubs around capabilities and time-zone coverage, while keeping policy, governance, data and performance management connected.

The operating model

Use clear global process owners, regional leaders with defined authority, common metrics, shared quality frameworks and structured mechanisms for transferring learning between locations.

The lesson

Globalization works when authority and accountability move together. A hub is not merely a lower-cost location; it is a durable source of leadership, resilience and institutional knowledge.
