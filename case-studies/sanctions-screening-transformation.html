Transformation Case Study

Rebuilding Sanctions Screening Around Effectiveness

How screening transformation can improve control quality, customer experience and operational efficiency together.

SanctionsScreeningCustomer experience
Public-safe case studyStrategic decisions, operating-model design and transferable lessons. Confidential detail is intentionally excluded.← All case studies

Sanctions screening programs can become overwhelmed by name variation, list expansion and poorly calibrated matching. High volume is not evidence of strong control; it can hide the risk that matters most.

The challenge

Legacy screening environments often produce large volumes of repetitive alerts, inconsistent list management and limited transparency into why a match was generated. Analysts spend time clearing noise while true risk competes for attention.

The strategic decision

Redesign screening around effectiveness: authoritative list management, calibrated matching, strong data normalization, clear disposition standards, quality assurance and rapid incorporation of geopolitical change.

Control qualityMore relevant matches and clearer rationale.
Customer experienceFewer avoidable interruptions and repeated reviews.
GovernanceControlled tuning, testing and list oversight.

The operating model

Global standards define required lists, matching governance, escalation and prohibited activity. Regional expertise helps interpret local names, transliteration and customer context. Data teams improve identity resolution, while quality and model governance monitor performance and unintended consequences.

What changed

Analyst capacity moves from repetitive clearing toward higher-value review. Customers experience fewer unnecessary delays, while the institution gains stronger evidence that matching logic and list coverage remain appropriate.

The lessons

  • Alert volume is not a proxy for sanctions effectiveness.
  • Name normalization and customer data quality are core controls.
  • Geopolitical change requires rapid, governed implementation.
  • Efficiency and effectiveness can improve together when noise is removed intelligently.
