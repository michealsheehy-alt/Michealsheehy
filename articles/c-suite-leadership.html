What Nobody Tells You About Joining the C-Suite

The decisions become less complete, the consequences become larger and the job becomes more human.

Micheal SheehyExecutive perspectives on payments, compliance and adaptive governance.← All insights

The biggest shift is not the title. It is moving from being rewarded for having answers to being accountable for decisions made before all the answers exist.

Being right is not the same as being influential

Executive leadership requires admitting when another person has the better plan, moving resources behind it and creating space for leaders to own outcomes.

The information is always incomplete

You rely on other people’s analysis, judgment and candor. The job is not to eliminate uncertainty. It is to understand which uncertainty matters and make a decision that the organization can execute.

It can be lonely

There are decisions you cannot crowdsource and feedback you must give even when it will disappoint someone. Leadership becomes less about approval and more about clarity, consistency and trust.

The most important capability is not projecting certainty. It is building a team strong enough to challenge you—and an organization resilient enough to succeed without needing you in every decision.
