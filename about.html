Executive profile

Micheal Sheehy

Micheal is a global compliance executive with more than two decades of experience across payments, banking and technology. He has led complex, multi-jurisdictional compliance organizations through growth, regulatory change and large-scale modernization.

His work focuses on translating regulatory expectations into scalable operating models: modernizing customer and transaction controls, building international teams, strengthening model governance and creating the infrastructure that allows businesses to grow with confidence.

His current writing explores a broader idea: that compliance must evolve from a collection of static controls into an adaptive intelligence system capable of learning as quickly as risk changes.
